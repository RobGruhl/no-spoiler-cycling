§ 01 · The race in 90 seconds

Evenepoel obliterates the field for a record fourth straight world TT title

OPENINGThe 39.2km course ran from Old Montréal out along the St Lawrence and through the Circuit Gilles Villeneuve before returning to the city for a rising finish into Parc Jeanne-Mance, with a steeper closing ramp around six percent. Remco Evenepoel, the three-time defending champion, was fastest through the first intermediate check in 12:17 — nine seconds up on under-23 world champion Jakob Söderqvist, with Filippo Ganna already 22 seconds back.

UNFOLDSGanna started slowly, sitting outside the top ten at the first check, while Stefan Küng also shipped time early. Evenepoel kept extending: by the second split he led Söderqvist by 34 seconds, with Ganna fourth and 40 seconds down. Ganna began recovering ground through the technical urban sector, moving into second at the third check but still 48 seconds behind Evenepoel, with Paul Seixas five seconds further back.

DECIDEDSöderqvist, in the leader's hot seat, crashed late on course and arrived at the finish bloodied, dropping him out of medal contention. Seixas moved into the provisional lead ahead of Ganna, who then took the fastest time to that point with a 16-second margin over the Frenchman — but both knew Evenepoel was still to come and had been fastest at every checkpoint.

FINALEEvenepoel powered up the closing ramp to Parc Jeanne-Mance and stopped the clock at 44:53.13, an average of 52.4km/h, finishing 57 seconds clear of Ganna and 1:13 ahead of Seixas. It was Evenepoel's fourth consecutive world time trial title — Glasgow, Zürich, Kigali and now Montréal — making him the first rider in the event's history (since 1994) to win four in a row.

🇧🇪 Remco Evenepoel Belgium

§ 06 · Aftermath

A record fourth straight rainbow jersey

""

—

Evenepoel's win makes him the first rider in the history of the World TT Championship (contested since 1994) to win four consecutive titles, surpassing the three-in-a-row records held by others and matching Fabian Cancellara and Tony Martin's four career wins overall — now in an unbroken run. He next turns his focus to the elite men's road race in the same city, aiming to become the first rider to win the TT and road race titles at the same Worlds.
