Vuelta a España 2026

§ 01 · The stage

Brennan doubles up in a reduced Catalan sprint

Fredrik Dversnes launched the day's break, soon joined by Mattia Gaffuri, Diego Uriarte, Walter Calzoni and Stefan Küng, and the five opened a lead of over two minutes before Küng abandoned the move with a mechanical. The peloton held the gap around a minute until echelons formed in the crosswinds at the halfway point, quickly reeling the remaining four attackers back in.

With the race back together, Ethan Hayter attacked roughly 15km from the intermediate sprint and took maximum points there, Wout van Aert crossing 22 seconds behind for second before Hayter sat up. Andreas Kron tried next, with 45km still to race, building a 42-second advantage that evaporated at the foot of the Alto de Paüls, the day's lone categorized climb, where Kaden Groves and Mads Pedersen were among those distanced from a peloton that crested roughly 60 riders strong.

Xabier Mikel Azparren attacked twice inside the final 20km; Van Aert closed the first move down and Christophe Laporte the second. From there Visma | Lease a Bike took control of the run-in and delivered a picture-perfect lead-out for Matthew Brennan, who came around to win the reduced bunch sprint in Roquetes for his second stage victory in the race's first five days, with Jordi Meeus and Vincenzo Albanese completing the podium.

§ 02 · GC impact

No change at the top — Pogačar still commands a 3:21 lead

The reduced sprint finish left the general classification untouched from stage 4: Tadej Pogačar retains the red jersey 3:21 ahead of Primož Roglič, with Enric Mas third at 3:32, Sepp Kuss fourth at 3:44 and Oscar Onley fifth at 3:45.

§ 04 · Rider-by-rider · tracked riders

Storylines from the stage

🏳️ Matthew Brennan Team Visma | Lease a Bike
LEADER P1

Perfectly delivered by his Visma | Lease a Bike train through the closing kilometres, Brennan came around the reduced bunch to win in Roquetes, his second stage victory of the race after also winning three days earlier. The 21-year-old Brit continues a breakout Grand Tour debut.

🇧🇪 Jordi Meeus Red Bull - BORA - hansgrohe
STAGE HUNTER P2 +0:00

Meeus finished second in the sprint, unable to match Brennan's lead-out advantage but confirming his form as one of the fastest men left in the reduced peloton.

🇧🇪 Wout Van Aert Team Visma | Lease a Bike
HELPER —

Van Aert was active throughout, taking second at the intermediate sprint behind a breakaway Ethan Hayter attack and later personally closing down Xabier Mikel Azparren's first late attack, before helping set up Brennan's winning sprint.

🇩🇪 Mads Pedersen Lidl - Trek
LEADER —

Pedersen was among the fast men dropped from the peloton on the day's lone climb, the Alto de Paüls, and missed the reduced group that contested the finish.
