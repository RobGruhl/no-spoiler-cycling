§ 01 · The stage

Magnier's foot slips off the pedal and Fretin steals the longest stage

The 212.3km run from Jingxi to Bama was the longest and toughest day of the race so far, with over 1,500m of climbing in its second half. Jasha Sütterlin, Tobias Bayer and Ruidong Wang went clear early and built a gap of nearly six minutes, with Wang mopping up the day's mountain points to take the polka-dot jersey from Afonso Eulálio. Soudal Quick-Step and Decathlon CMA CGM Team drove the chase through the categorised climbs, and the escapees were swept up with roughly 19km to go as the bunch crested the final climb together with 13km remaining, setting up a bunch sprint. XDS Astana Team led the technical run into Bama for Max Kanter, with Paul Magnier tucked in behind in position to contest a second stage win of the week. In the final 150 metres, Magnier's foot came out of the pedal as he accelerated, ending his chance instantly. Milan Fretin jumped through the gap to take the win, holding off a fast-finishing Frits Biesterbos — the previous day's winner — with Jordi Meeus completing the podium and Fabio Jakobsen narrowly missing it in fourth for his best result since joining Visma | Lease a Bike. The whole field crossed in the same time as the winner.

§ 02 · GC impact

Biesterbos takes the race lead on countback from Magnier

Frits Biesterbos moves into the overall lead, level on time with Paul Magnier after three stages but ahead on the sum of his daily placings. Max Kanter sits third overall, 10 seconds back. Magnier's run of stage wins and the overall lead he held since stage 1 ends here, though he remains very much in contention given the time gap is nominal.

§ 04 · Rider-by-rider · tracked riders

Storylines from the stage

🇫🇷 Paul Magnier Soudal Quick-Step
LEADER P21 m.t.

Magnier was perfectly positioned off Kanter's lead-out and in position to contest a second stage win of the week when his foot slipped off the pedal mid-acceleration inside the final 150 metres, ending his sprint before it started. He finished safely in the bunch, losing no time, but the mishap cost him the stage and, on the counting-back tiebreak, the overall lead he'd held since stage 1.

🇧🇪 Jordi Meeus Red Bull - BORA - hansgrohe
STAGE HUNTER P3 m.t.

Meeus timed his effort well through the chaotic finish, coming through cleanly as Magnier faltered ahead of him to take his second podium in three days, this time third behind Fretin and Biesterbos.

🇳🇱 Olav Kooij Decathlon CMA CGM Team
STAGE HUNTER P64 m.t.

Kooij was boxed out of position on the technical run into Bama and never featured in the sprint, finishing deep in the bunch on the same time as the winner after two days of close misses.
