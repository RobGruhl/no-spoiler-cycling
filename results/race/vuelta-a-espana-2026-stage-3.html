§ S3 · MOUNTAIN · 166 km

Stage 3: Gruissan → Font Romeu
Vuelta a España 2026

Stage 3's first summit finish at Font Romeu was cancelled with roughly 15-20km still to race after an intense hailstorm hit the Col de Mont-Louis climb, forcing riders to shelter roadside; no winner or podium was declared and the general classification carries over unchanged from Stage 2.

Stage podium
§ 01 · The stage

Hailstorm halts the Vuelta's first mountain stage short of the finish

The 174km run from Gruissan to Font Romeu stayed flat until the closing climbs, with the route rising into the Cat. 1 Col de Mont-Louis and on toward the Font Romeu summit finish. Heavy rain began falling around 20km from the line, quickly turning to hail as the race hit the mountain classification point on Mont-Louis.

Organisers first neutralised the race on the climb as conditions worsened, with riders taking shelter in shops, restaurants and hotels along the roadside. With the hail intensifying and the descent and finishing climb judged too dangerous to continue, race officials cancelled the remainder of the stage entirely, with roughly 15-20km still left to race.

Race organisers confirmed there would be no declared stage winner and no podium ceremony, and that the stage would not count toward the general classification.

§ 02 · GC impact

GC frozen — Pogačar's lead carries over from Stage 2

With no times recorded past the cancellation point, the general classification reverts to the Stage 2 standings: Tadej Pogačar retains the red jersey, 9 seconds ahead of Wout van Aert and 10 seconds ahead of Matthew Brennan. The loss of the Font Romeu summit finish removes the Vuelta's first real climbing test before the race reaches the Pyrenees again later in the route.
