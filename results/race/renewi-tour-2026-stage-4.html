§ 01 · The stage

Philipsen edges Kooij again to snatch back the lead before Leuven

The 187.3 km run from Riemst to Bilzen-Hoeselt started with a Green Kilometre sprint won by Lotto Intermarché's Lionel Taminiaux, protecting overnight leader Jenno Berckmoes's slender six-second cushion. Soren Kragh Andersen (Lidl-Trek) attacked after the intermediate sprints and opened a 17-second gap, forcing Netcompany INEOS to organise the chase; he survived being nearly caught on the Letenberg to carry a 12-second lead into the closing two kilometres.

On the finishing climb, Arnaud De Lie sacrificed his own chances to accelerate for Berckmoes, and Florian Vermeersch drove the pace further, briefly splitting the bunch before Philipsen and Kooij regained contact. Unibet Rose Rockets closed the gap to Kragh Andersen inside the final 600 metres, setting up a bunch finish. Biniam Girmay launched the sprint with Tim Merlier tracking Philipsen's wheel and Dylan Groenewegen also in contention, before Philipsen and Kooij emerged together on the rising finish and threw their bikes for the line.

The photo finish confirmed Philipsen's win in 3:57:45, his second of the race, with Kooij given the same time. Because Lotto Intermarché's earlier efforts had prevented Philipsen and Kooij from taking bonus seconds at the intermediate sprints, Berckmoes's team could not fully protect the lead once Kragh Andersen was caught, and Philipsen's stage-winning bonus seconds moved him back into the blue-green leader's jersey ahead of Sunday's final stage in Leuven.

§ 02 · GC impact

Philipsen retakes the overall lead by 4 seconds

Jasper Philipsen leads by 4 seconds over Jenno Berckmoes, with Olav Kooij third at +10. Quinten Hermans is fourth at +23, Mike Teunissen fifth at +26, Alec Segaert sixth at +27, and Soren Waerenskjold seventh, also at +27. Matteo Trentin is eighth at +28, Robert Donaldson ninth at +29, and Filippo Fiorelli rounds out the top ten at +30, with Sunday's final stage in Leuven still to decide the race.

Storylines from the stage

LEADER P1

Philipsen tracked the closing moves before launching alongside Kooij on the rising finish, throwing his bike for a photo-finish win — his second stage victory of the race. The bonus seconds moved him back into the overall lead by four seconds over Berckmoes with one hilly stage left in Leuven.

🇳🇱 Olav Kooij Decathlon CMA CGM Team
STAGE HUNTER P2 +0:00

Kooij matched Philipsen's finishing kick and was given the same time in the photo finish, but the ranking cost him the overall lead pursuit for now — he sits third overall, ten seconds back, heading into Sunday's final stage.

🇪🇷 Biniam Girmay NSN Cycling Team
STAGE HUNTER P7 +0:00

Girmay launched the sprint from distance in the closing metres, opening the effort that Philipsen and Kooij ultimately came around, and finished seventh in the same bunch time.

STAGE HUNTER P11 +0:00

Merlier sat on Philipsen's wheel through the finale but couldn't get around him in the sprint, finishing 11th in the same time as the winner.
