Stage 8: Puçol → Xeraco
Vuelta a España 2026

Bryan Coquard produced the sprint of his career to beat Mads Pedersen and Jordi Meeus in a crash-scrambled finish at Xeraco, but the stage will be remembered for Tadej Pogačar's race-ending crash on the run-in to the Puerto de Barx — the Slovenian abandoned with a suspected broken collarbone, handing the red jersey to Enric Mas.

§ 01 · The stage

Coquard's sprint upset overshadowed by Pogačar's shock abandon

Sinuhé Fernández attacked from the flag and built a lead of almost six minutes before Visma | Lease a Bike took control at the front and reeled him in ahead of the intermediate sprint at Simat de la Valldigna, where Wout van Aert added to his points-classification lead. Roughly 32km from the finish, at the foot of the Puerto de Barx, Tadej Pogačar hit the tarmac hard in a peloton crash that also caught Ben Tulett. He got up and tried to continue but couldn't, and was taken away by ambulance with a head injury and a suspected broken collarbone, ending his Vuelta.

The bunch briefly waited before racing resumed once it was clear the race leader was out. Van Aert crested the Puerto de Barx first for more mountains points, and Xabier Mikel Azparren attacked on the descent, opening a gap of around 20 seconds that the bunch closed down with about 1.5km still to race.

From there the finish unravelled into chaos. Visma | Lease a Bike again built the leadout around two-time stage winner Matthew Brennan, with Christophe Laporte and Van Aert driving the front, but Brennan lost his teammates' wheel inside the final kilometre. A crash just before the line was followed by another pile-up inside the finishing straight, scattering riders and bikes across the road. Coquard emerged from the survivors to time his effort perfectly, coming around Pedersen right on the line for his biggest win in years, with Meeus completing the podium.

With Pogačar's overnight abandon, Enric Mas inherits the red jersey for the first time in his career, and a Vuelta that looked settled is thrown wide open.

§ 02 · GC impact

Mas inherits red as Pogačar's 3:50 overnight lead vanishes

With Pogačar's time struck from the classification, Enric Mas becomes the new race leader for the first time in his career. Primož Roglič is second at 1:00 and Felix Gall third at 1:11 — the two finished together in the bunch, so the 11-second gap between them from before the stage carries over unchanged. Sepp Kuss is fourth at 2:15, Oscar Onley fifth at 2:16, Richard Carapaz sixth at 2:22, Mattias Skjelmose seventh at 3:27, Harold Tejada eighth at 3:56, Gregor Mühlberger ninth at 5:39 and Jakob Omrzel tenth at 6:04.

§ 04 · Rider-by-rider · tracked riders

Storylines from the stage

🇸🇮 Tadej Pogačar UAE Team Emirates - XRG
LEADER INCIDENT DNF

Pogačar crashed hard in the peloton roughly 32km from the finish on the approach to the Puerto de Barx, in an incident that also caught Ben Tulett. He got back up and tried to continue but couldn't carry on, and was taken away by ambulance with a head injury and a suspected broken collarbone. He had arrived at the stage with a 3:50 overall lead and three stage wins already banked in the first week; the abandon ends his 2026 Grand Tour campaign there and hands the red jersey to Enric Mas.
