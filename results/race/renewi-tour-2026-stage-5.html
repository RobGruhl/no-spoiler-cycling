§ 01 · The stage

Philipsen doubles up in Leuven to seal the overall title

The final 183 km stage started and finished in Leuven on a circuit mirroring the 2021 World Championships course, with Dries De Bondt, Hartthijs de Vries, Robert Stannard, Frederik Frison, Emil Herzog and Edward Theuns forming the day's breakaway. Alpecin - Premier Tech controlled the pace behind as the escapees built a lead of several minutes before being reeled in.

With the GC still live, Florian Vermeersch attacked 20 km from the line, and Jenno Berckmoes followed to test Philipsen's overall lead. Tomas Kopecky and Pavel Bittner joined the move with just over 7 km remaining before the bunch came back together, and Berckmoes attacked Philipsen once more, only for the Belgian to respond immediately.

Inside the final 2 km, Matej Mohorič and an Uno-X Mobility rider attacked as a crash behind thinned the peloton and affected Biniam Girmay. With Astana driving the pace and no team able to organise a chase, the Uno-X rider was caught in sight of the line and Philipsen came around to win his second straight sprint of the week, securing the overall Renewi Tour title with the stage bonus seconds.

§ 02 · GC impact

Philipsen wins the Renewi Tour overall

Jasper Philipsen wins the final general classification by 14 seconds over Jenno Berckmoes, with Olav Kooij third at +0:29. Mike Teunissen moves up to fourth at +0:32 and Quinten Hermans is fifth at +0:33. Matteo Trentin, Robert Donaldson, Filippo Fiorelli, Marijn van den Berg and Tim van Dijke round out the top ten; Alec Segaert and Søren Wærenskjold both fell out of the top ten after crashing the day before. Philipsen also won the points classification.
