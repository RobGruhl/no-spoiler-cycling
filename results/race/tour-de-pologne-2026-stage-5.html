§ 01 · The stage

Christen out-climbs the field, Lemmen defends yellow on 19% ramps

A seven-rider breakaway of Rémi Cavagna, Paul Magnier, Patrick Gamper, Filip Maciejuk, Taco van der Hoorn, Tim Naberman and mountains leader Dries De Bondt built a gap of just under two minutes on a mostly flat run from Opole, with Maciejuk later dropping out after a puncture. The advantage evaporated on the first categorised climb to Przełęcz Przegibek as the peloton's GC teams wound up the pace, and the escapees were swept up in the finale.

The decisive test came on the short, brutally steep finishing climb into Kocierz Resort, 1.2-1.5km at an average gradient of 11.5% ramping to nearly 20% in places. The fight for position going in was fierce, and Neilson Powless set a pace on the lower slopes too hard for explosive attacks. With 2.1km to go, Jan Christen accelerated clear with no immediate response, even from race leader Bart Lemmen's group.

In the false-flat run to the line, Marco Brenner closed hard from the GC group and briefly passed Christen, who had eased up expecting a sprint. Christen found a last surge in the final uphill ramp to retake Brenner and hold on for his first WorldTour stage victory, with Louis Barré completing the podium four seconds back. Lemmen, defending yellow on terrain that suited the climbers far more than him, finished 11th in the group five seconds behind the winner to keep his overall lead intact.

§ 02 · GC impact

Lemmen holds yellow; Christen and Brenner climb into the GC top four

Bart Lemmen retains the overall lead, six seconds ahead of Christian Scaroni in second. Marco Brenner moves up to third at 15 seconds after matching the stage winner's time, and stage winner Jan Christen jumps to fourth overall at 18 seconds having not been a GC factor before Kocierz Resort. João Almeida, the pre-race favourite who lost time in the stage 4 crash, did not start stage 5 and is out of the race.

§ 04 · Rider-by-rider · tracked riders

Storylines from the stage

🇨🇭 Jan Christen UAE Team Emirates - XRG
LEADER P1 —

Christen launched the decisive move 2.1km from the summit finish at Kocierz Resort, opening a gap that nobody in the GC group could close. He briefly lost the initiative when Marco Brenner overtook him on the false-flat run-in, having eased up too early, but found one more surge in the final ramp to retake the German and hold on for his first WorldTour victory. The result vaults him from outside GC contention into fourth overall, 18 seconds behind race leader Bart Lemmen, giving UAE Team Emirates-XRG a GC card to play after João Almeida's abandonment.

🇮🇹 Christian Scaroni XDS Astana Team
LEADER P9 s.t.

Scaroni followed the key GC wheels up the Kocierz Resort finale and finished safely in the group behind the stage winner, taking no time losses. He remains second overall, six seconds behind Bart Lemmen, keeping himself in the GC fight with two stages remaining.

🇵🇹 João Almeida UAE Team Emirates - XRG
LEADER INCIDENT DNS

Almeida did not start stage 5, unable to recover from the leg injury sustained in the mass crash during stage 4. UAE Team Emirates-XRG confirmed his withdrawal from the race the morning of the stage, along with teammates Benoît Cosnefroy and Sebastian Molano, ending his overall bid at this year's Tour de Pologne.
