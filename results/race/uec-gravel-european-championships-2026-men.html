2026

Mads Würtz Schmidt (Denmark) defended his European gravel title in Houffalize, riding solo off the front of a four-rider break before the final lap to win by 1:09, with Belgians Wout Alleman and Niels Vandeputte taking silver and bronze.

§ 01 · The race in 90 seconds

Würtz Schmidt solos to a second straight European gravel title

OPENINGOn the technically demanding climbing course in the Ardennes around Houffalize, a four-rider group containing Würtz Schmidt and Wout Alleman broke clear as early as the first of three laps of the course.

UNFOLDSThe move held its advantage through the second lap while the rest of the group's other two escapees gradually faded back toward the chase. Approaching the start of the final lap, Würtz Schmidt rode away from his remaining companions alone.

DECIDEDThe Danish rider, considered the strongest gravel specialist of the moment after also winning Unbound Gravel earlier this season, extended his solo advantage across the closing lap of the 138km race.

FINALEWürtz Schmidt crossed the line alone in 4h09'57" to retain his European title, 1:09 clear of the field. Behind him, marathon mountain biker Wout Alleman held on for second, while cyclocross rider Niels Vandeputte (Alpecin-Premier Tech) took bronze at 3:43, just ahead of Frenchman Eddy Le Huitouze. Floris De Tier finished ninth; reigning Belgian national champion Daan Soete was not a factor in the finale.

§ 06 · Aftermath

Würtz Schmidt confirms his status as gravel's dominant rider

The win adds a second consecutive European title to a season in which the 32-year-old former WorldTour pro has also won Unbound Gravel, the sport's biggest one-day race outside the world championships. For the host nation, Alleman and Vandeputte's podium finishes gave Belgium two of the three medals in front of the home Houffalize crowd.
