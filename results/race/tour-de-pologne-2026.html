§ 01 · The race in 90 seconds

Milan seizes the yellow jersey on the opening day in Koszalin

OPENINGThe 83rd Tour de Pologne began in Gdynia with its longest stage of the week, a 234km run down the Baltic coast and through Kashubia to Koszalin. A six-rider breakaway featuring home favourites Kamil Malecki and Radoslaw Fratczak built a lead of around two and a half minutes before the sprinters' teams began organising the chase.

UNFOLDSMalecki mopped up intermediate sprints and bonus seconds through his home region, including a pass through his home town of Bytow, and grabbed the most-combative jersey for his efforts. The breakaway was caught with around 17km to go, prompting an immediate solo counter from Dries De Bondt, who took the day's only KOM points atop Gora Chelmska before being swept up inside the final 6km.

DECIDEDDecathlon CMA CGM Team lifted the pace in the closing kilometres as the bunch came back together at speed, setting up a mass sprint finish in Koszalin.

FINALEJonathan Milan timed his effort perfectly from around 300m out, holding off Paul Magnier at the line with Noah Hobbs third. The win gives Milan the yellow, points and combined leads heading into stage 2.

§ 06 · Aftermath

One stage down, six to go

After the opening 234km stage from Gdynia to Koszalin, Jonathan Milan leads the general classification, points classification and combined classification. Dries De Bondt holds the mountains jersey after the day's lone KOM effort, and Kamil Malecki leads the most-combative classification. The race continues with a flat stage to Szczecin on day two.
