§ 01 · The race in 90 seconds

Pogačar opens his home Vuelta with a photo-finish time trial win, then survives a chaotic Manosque sprint

OPENINGThe 2026 Vuelta a España began in Monaco with a short, technical 9.4 km individual time trial featuring 25 corners, a course built to reward explosive, punchy riders over pure specialists against the clock. A string of young riders — Jordan Labrosse, Matthew Brennan, Oscar Chamberlain, Léo Bisiaux and Callum Thornley — briefly held the fastest times through the afternoon before Netcompany INEOS's Joshua Tarling, competing despite the recent death of his younger brother, set a new benchmark of 11:01.

UNFOLDSNational time-trial champion Ethan Hayter went four seconds quicker than Tarling to take the provisional lead, with Christophe Laporte, Wout van Aert and Finn Fisher-Black all close behind but unable to match it. Starting second-to-last on streets he calls home, Tadej Pogačar edged out Hayter by nine hundredths of a second at the line, taking both the stage and the Vuelta's first red jersey. Primož Roglič, starting last, conceded 22 seconds to his fellow Slovenian. Stage 2 ran largely flat from Monaco to Manosque until Hayter attacked from the day's breakaway with 49 km to go, briefly moving into the provisional overall lead on bonus seconds before being caught. Inside the final kilometre of an uphill finish, Wout van Aert attacked in a bid for the jersey, drawing Pogačar's response, before the move was reeled in and Grand Tour debutant Matthew Brennan out-sprinted the field to win his first Grand Tour stage. Pogačar held third and kept the red jersey as Hayter arrived off the back of the sprint.

DECIDEDNot yet decided. Two of the race's roughly three weeks of stages are complete; Pogačar holds a single-digit-second overall lead with the race yet to reach the mountains of Andorra and Spain.

FINALENot yet raced. The route continues through France, Andorra and Spain before finishing in Madrid on 13 September.

Where the race tilted

  1. 01 Monaco (Stage 1, ITT)
    Pogačar wins the opening time trial by 0.09 seconds
    On a technical, corner-heavy 9.4 km course through Monaco, Tadej Pogačar started second-to-last and edged out Ethan Hayter by nine hundredths of a second to take the stage and the race's first red jersey.
  2. 02 1 km to go Manosque (Stage 2)
    Van Aert's late attack for the jersey is caught before the line
    Wout van Aert attacked out of a hairpin inside the final kilometre with Pogačar responding directly, but the move was reeled in under the 1 km banner, setting up a bunch sprint won by Matthew Brennan.

§ 06 · Aftermath

Two stages down, red jersey retained

As of the evening of 23 August 2026, after stage 2, Tadej Pogačar leads the Vuelta a España by 9 seconds over Wout van Aert and 10 over Matthew Brennan, having held the red jersey he won in the Monaco time trial through a chaotic uphill sprint finish in Manosque. GC riders João Almeida and Felix Gall lost 18 seconds in a split on the finishing climb. The race continues through France, Andorra and Spain before finishing in Madrid on 13 September.
