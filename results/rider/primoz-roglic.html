Vuelta a España
GRAND TOUR
LEADER P2 +1:37 · Red Bull - BORA - hansgrohe

Roglič conceded 22 seconds in the Monaco opener and 2:39 to Pogačar's Andorra raid, and was distanced again on the Bartolo gravel and at Valdelinares. Pogačar's abandon lifted him to second overall, and Enric Mas dropped him twice more, on Aitana and Calar Alto, before he lost the runner-up spot itself on stage 14: closing late on Carapaz's move on the Sierra de la Pandera, he was repeatedly caught out in the finale and conceded 25 seconds to Mas and Gall, dropping to third. The heat-shortened stage 15 and the bunch sprints on stages 16 and 17 left the gap unchanged, but Roglič answered on stage 18, riding the most aggressive time trial of the GC contenders to take 33 seconds out of Mas and leapfrog back into second overall at 1:37. He tested the red jersey again with a late attack on Peñas Blancas on stage 19, but Mas covered it immediately and the group finished together, leaving the gap unchanged with only the Collado del Alguacil left to find real time.
