§ 01 · The season so far

Arc

Oscar Onley brought his GC ambitions to the Tour Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes (the rebranded Critérium du Dauphiné) in June with the team racing as Netcompany–INEOS. He sat in the top ten through the first half — comfortably in the front group on the stage-1 hilly finish at Saint-Ismier and well placed after INEOS's strong stage-3 team time trial — before the high mountains and the race-altering stage-6 split moved him down the order. The week served as a Tour de France build-up rather than a result in itself, with the team's GC focus ultimately split across Onley and Kévin Vauquelin.

Vuelta a España — Stage 12: Vera–Calar Alto → Calar Alto
GRAND TOUR
LEADER P12 +5:05 · Netcompany INEOS Cycling Team

Onley's day was compromised early, sliding out on a roundabout with 131km still to race and having to chase back to the bunch behind the team car with his white jersey covered in dirt. He recovered from the crash but was dropped when Felix Gall accelerated 13km from the Calar Alto summit, unable to follow Mas, Roglič and Gall as the GC group split. Teammate Jack Haig, who had been up the road in the day's breakaway, waited to pace him up much of the final climb, and Onley limited the damage to finish 12th on the stage, but the combination of the crash and the late split cost him more than two minutes to Mas and dropped him from fourth to fifth overall.
