01 · The Problem

The front page of the internet will tell you who won.

Cycling fans who can't watch live face a real dilemma: finding race recordings online means risking spoilers. A YouTube thumbnail, a video title, a sidebar recommendation — any of them can ruin a five-hour Grand Tour stage or a nail-biting Monument classic before you've clipped in.

02 · The Solution

A curated roadbook of professional races, with verified-safe links.

Every race on the calendar has been evaluated for spoiler-safe viewing. Full replays, extended highlights that save the result for the natural finish, and broadcaster links by region — so you can watch the race unfold, not the headline.

a.Full Replays

Start-to-finish broadcast feeds where licensed. FloBikes, Discovery+, Peacock, Max, TNT Sports, SBS, 7plus, and national broadcaster YouTube channels.

b.Safe Highlights

Extended highlight cuts where the winner is revealed only at the natural finish — chronological, neutral title, no post-race interviews bleeding through.

c.Where-to-Watch

Broadcast rights by geography. Deep links into the event page where possible; clearly labelled login-walled root URLs where not.

03 · Who's Behind It

Two fans who watch on delay.

This is a two-person project — my girlfriend and I love watching bike racing together, and almost never live. Grand Tour stages finish while we're at work; the Classics finish before we've had breakfast. Watching on our schedule meant living in fear of push alerts, thumbnails, podium photos, and well-meaning friends.

So we built the calendar we wanted: every race worth watching, when it happens, where to legally watch it in your country, and replay links that have actually been checked so the winner is revealed the way it should be — at the finish line, after five hours of racing, not in a video title.

It's a labor of love, not a business. No ads, no accounts, no tracking scripts — just a roadbook we use ourselves, every week of the season.

04 · House Rules

How we decide a link is safe.

One rule governs everything: the winner is revealed only at the natural finish. Every replay and highlight link on the calendar side is held to it.

i.Neutral titles only

"Stage 15 — Full Coverage" is safe. "What a ride by [rider] on Stage 15!" is not. If the title, description, or thumbnail leans toward the result, the link doesn't ship.

ii.Chronological cuts

Highlights must run in race order with no podium shots, winner interviews, or celebration frames bleeding in before the finish.

iii.When in doubt, leave it out

A missing highlight is a non-event; a leaked result is the worst thing this site can do. We skip anything we can't verify.

iv.Results live behind a gate

Post-race analysis exists on the site — but only behind a clearly-marked spoiler gate that remembers your choice. You'll never trip over a result by accident.

05 · Under the Hood

A static site with no strings attached.

Plain HTML on GitHub Pages — no backend, no database, no accounts, no analytics scripts. The whole thing is open source, and the curation is honest work: broadcast rights get verified against independent listings per country, and every footage link passes a spoiler review before it renders. We use AI tools for the research legwork — checking rights, finding replays across hundreds of races — but the standards above are ours, and the site exists because we wanted to use it.

06 · Talk to Us

Spot something wrong? Tell us.

  • 01
    A leaked result anywhere on the calendar side — please report it immediately via GitHub issues. That's the one failure we truly care about.
  • 02
    A wrong broadcaster for your country — rights move around constantly (ask us about the week a race showed FloBikes but streamed on Max). Corrections welcome.
  • 03
    A race we're missing — if it's on the UCI calendar and worth watching, we want it on here.
The Promise

You can open this calendar the morning after any race, find out how to watch it, and still experience every attack, every crosswind split, and every sprint as if it were live. That's the whole point.
